Paper Cut explores how born-digital cultural heritage can be preserved, accessed, and interpreted across technological shifts. This project brings together archival theory, media archaeology, and critical infrastructure studies to examine the material and political conditions of digital memory. It operates as a long-term research platform that develops both conceptual frameworks and practical prototypes for sustainable digital preservation.

The project is structured around three core axes:

1.  Infrastructure – How digital archives are built, maintained, and governed.

2.  Access – Who can access digital archives, and under what conditions.

3.  Interpretation – How meaning is produced through archival encounters in digital environments.

The initiative is hosted within the Journal of Incomplete Studies and runs across Season 04 and beyond.
