<div class="section-wrap">
  <div class="col-head">
    <span class="col-head-title">How it works</span>
    <span class="col-head-meta">A seasonal system for thinking in time</span>
  </div>
  <div class="how-body">
    <div class="how-intro">
      <p>The Unfinished Archive is not a journal. It is a <em>system</em> — a structured framework for developing thought over time, publishing it in serial form, and allowing it to remain open. Each component of that system is described here.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="how-phases">
      <div class="how-phase">
        <div class="how-phase-num">1</div>
        <div class="how-phase-label">Phase 01 · Month 1</div>
        <div class="how-phase-title">Selection</div>
        <div class="how-phase-body">Projects are selected each season through an open call. 3–6 projects form the seasonal cohort, spanning disciplines and methods.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="how-phase">
        <div class="how-phase-num">2</div>
        <div class="how-phase-label">Phase 02 · Months 1–4</div>
        <div class="how-phase-title">Publication</div>
        <div class="how-phase-body">Contributors publish monthly installments. Each installment is a discrete piece of writing — not a chapter, but an intervention.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="how-phase">
        <div class="how-phase-num">3</div>
        <div class="how-phase-label">Phase 03 · Monthly</div>
        <div class="how-phase-title">Development</div>
        <div class="how-phase-body">Writers engage with editorial feedback and with each other's work. Revision is encouraged. Contradiction is permitted. Return is built in.</div>
      </div>
      <div class="how-phase">
        <div class="how-phase-num">4</div>
        <div class="how-phase-label">Phase 04 · End of cycle</div>
        <div class="how-phase-title">Resolution</div>
        <div class="how-phase-body">Projects may conclude, continue, or be developed into a completed work through Second Form Editions. None of these is the default outcome.</div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="annotation">
      <div class="annotation-label">Core principle</div>
      <div class="annotation-text">The installment is not a draft. It is a published position. If it is later revised or retracted, the original remains visible. The archive is cumulative, not corrected.</div>
    </div>

    <div class="how-principles">
      <div>
        <div class="how-principle-title">What an installment is</div>
        <div class="how-principle-body">An installment is a self-contained piece of writing of 1,500–4,000 words. It may open a question, develop a previous position, or complicate one. It is published with a date, a version marker, and its place in the project sequence. It is always presented in the context of what came before.<br><br>The installment is not a chapter. It is not working toward a conclusion that exists elsewhere. The installment is the work — the form and the content are the same thing.</div>
      </div>
      <div>
        <div class="how-principle-title">What the archive accumulates</div>
        <div class="how-principle-body">Over time, the site becomes a record of <em>thought in motion</em> — showing not only what contributors concluded, but how they moved, what they revised, what they abandoned, and what they returned to.<br><br>Strikethroughs are not corrections. They are retractions left visible — the earlier position remains readable alongside its revision. The archive is a record of the movement of thought, not a collection of its outcomes.</div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
