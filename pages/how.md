    <div class="content" style="padding: 2rem 0; border-bottom: 1px solid var(--rule); margin-bottom: 3rem;">
      <p>The Unfinished Archive is not a journal. It is a <em>system</em> — a structured framework for developing thought over time, publishing it in serial form, and allowing it to remain open. Each component of that system is described here.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="grid-4" style="border: 1px solid var(--rule-mid); margin-bottom: 3rem;">
      <div class="how-phase" style="padding: 2rem 1.5rem; border-right: 1px solid var(--rule); position: relative; overflow: hidden;">

        <div class="text-mono" style="font-size: 8px; letter-spacing: 0.18em; text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--gold-dim); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Phase 01 · Month 1</div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 18px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--cream); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1; line-height: 1.2;">Selection</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 12.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--muted); line-height: 1.7; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Projects are selected each season through an open call. 3–6 projects form the seasonal cohort, spanning disciplines and methods.</div>
      </div>
      
      <div class="how-phase" style="padding: 2rem 1.5rem; border-right: 1px solid var(--rule); position: relative; overflow: hidden;">
        <span class="text-display" style="font-size: 80px; font-weight: 900; color: var(--void2); line-height: 1; position: absolute; top: -0.1em; right: -0.05em; pointer-events: none; letter-spacing: -0.04em;">2</span>
        <div class="text-mono" style="font-size: 8px; letter-spacing: 0.18em; text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--gold-dim); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Phase 02 · Months 1–4</div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 18px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--cream); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1; line-height: 1.2;">Publication</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 12.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--muted); line-height: 1.7; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Contributors publish monthly installments. Each installment is a discrete piece of writing — not a chapter, but an intervention.</div>
      </div>
      
      <div class="how-phase" style="padding: 2rem 1.5rem; border-right: 1px solid var(--rule); position: relative; overflow: hidden;">
        <span class="text-display" style="font-size: 80px; font-weight: 900; color: var(--void2); line-height: 1; position: absolute; top: -0.1em; right: -0.05em; pointer-events: none; letter-spacing: -0.04em;">3</span>
        <div class="text-mono" style="font-size: 8px; letter-spacing: 0.18em; text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--gold-dim); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Phase 03 · Monthly</div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 18px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--cream); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1; line-height: 1.2;">Development</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 12.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--muted); line-height: 1.7; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Writers engage with editorial feedback and with each other's work. Revision is encouraged. Contradiction is permitted. Return is built in.</div>
      </div>

        <div class="text-mono" style="font-size: 8px; letter-spacing: 0.18em; text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--gold-dim); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Phase 04 · End of cycle</div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 18px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--cream); margin-bottom: 0.6rem; position: relative; z-index: 1; line-height: 1.2;">Resolution</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 12.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--muted); line-height: 1.7; position: relative; z-index: 1;">Projects may conclude, continue, or be developed into a completed work through Second Form Editions. None of these is the default outcome.</div>
      </div>

    <div class="annotation" style="border-left: 2px solid var(--gold-dim); padding: 1rem 1.2rem; background: rgba(200, 169, 110, 0.04); margin: 2rem 0; max-width: 640px;">
      <div class="text-mono" style="font-size: 8px; letter-spacing: 0.16em; text-transform: uppercase; color: var(--gold-dim); margin-bottom: 0.4rem;">Core principle</div>
      <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 13px; font-style: italic; color: var(--pale); line-height: 1.75;">The installment is not a draft. It is a published position. If it is later revised or retracted, the original remains visible. The archive is cumulative, not corrected.</div>
    </div>

    <div class="grid-2" style="gap: 3rem; margin-top: 3rem;">
      <div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 20px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--bright); margin-bottom: 1rem; padding-bottom: 0.6rem; border-bottom: 1px solid var(--rule);">What an installment is</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 13.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--pale); line-height: 1.85;">An installment is a self-contained piece of writing of 1,500–4,000 words. It may open a question, develop a previous position, or complicate one. It is published with a date, a version marker, and its place in the project sequence. It is always presented in the context of what came before.<br><br>The installment is not a chapter. It is not working toward a conclusion that exists elsewhere. The installment is the work — the form and the content are the same thing.</div>
      </div>
      <div>
        <div class="text-display" style="font-size: 20px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 700; color: var(--bright); margin-bottom: 1rem; padding-bottom: 0.6rem; border-bottom: 1px solid var(--rule);">What the archive accumulates</div>
        <div class="text-text" style="font-size: 13.5px; font-style: italic; color: var(--pale); line-height: 1.85;">Over time, the site becomes a record of <em>thought in motion</em> — showing not only what contributors concluded, but how they moved, what they revised, what they abandoned, and what they returned to.<br><br>Strikethroughs are not corrections. They are retractions left visible — the earlier position remains readable alongside its revision. The archive is a record of the movement of thought, not a collection of its outcomes.</div>
      </div>
