<div class="section-wrap">
  <div class="col-head">
    <span class="col-head-title">For Contributors</span>
    <span class="col-head-meta">Open call · Season 02 opens Spring 2026</span>
  </div>
  <div class="cr-body">
    <div class="cr-lead">
      The Unfinished Archive is for writers who are <em>mid-thought</em> — working at the edge of a question that is not yet ready to be resolved. The format is designed to support exactly this condition.
    </div>

    <div class="cr-two-col">
      <div>
        <div class="cr-block-title">Who it is for</div>
        <ul class="cr-list">
          <li>Doctoral candidates developing dissertation-adjacent writing</li>
          <li>Early-career researchers between institutional projects</li>
          <li>Independent scholars without access to traditional publication channels</li>
          <li>Established researchers seeking space for provisional, process-oriented work</li>
          <li>Writers whose work crosses disciplinary boundaries in ways that resist single-journal publication</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div>
        <div class="cr-block-title">What contributors do</div>
        <ul class="cr-list">
          <li>Submit a project proposal with an initial installment at application</li>
          <li>Publish one installment per month for the duration of the seasonal cycle</li>
          <li>Engage with editorial response — not revision directives, but dialogue</li>
          <li>Participate in a seasonal conversation with the cohort</li>
          <li>Maintain the project's integrity while allowing it to change</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="cr-callout">
      <div class="cr-callout-title">What contributors receive</div>
      <div class="cr-callout-text">Publication in a structured, publicly accessible archive. Editorial development across the season. Access to the cohort's reading and response. And, for projects that reach appropriate completion, a pathway into <em>Second Form Editions</em> — the publication arm that develops archive projects into finished books.</div>
    </div>

    <div class="annotation" style="margin-top:2.5rem">
      <div class="annotation-label">On the nature of the commitment</div>
      <div class="annotation-text">This is not a journal submission. You are not handing over a finished text. You are committing to a process — to writing in public, revising in public, and remaining with a question longer than is usual. The format asks for duration, not volume.</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
