<div class="section-wrap">
  <div class="col-head">
    <span class="col-head-title">For Readers</span>
    <span class="col-head-meta">How to follow a line of thought</span>
  </div>
  <div class="cr-body">
    <div class="cr-lead">
      The Unfinished Archive is not read like a journal. You do not wait for an issue and read it through. You <em>follow projects</em> — returning to writers whose questions interest you, month after month, watching thought develop in real time.
    </div>

    <div class="cr-two-col">
      <div>
        <div class="cr-block-title">How reading works here</div>
        <ul class="cr-list">
          <li>Each project publishes monthly; new installments appear in the Installments feed</li>
          <li>You can enter a project at any installment — no obligation to begin at the start</li>
          <li>Each installment links back to earlier positions in the same project</li>
          <li>You follow writers, not issues — the project, not the volume</li>
          <li>Revisions and retractions remain visible; you see thought changing, not just its outcomes</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div>
        <div class="cr-block-title">What the experience offers</div>
        <ul class="cr-list">
          <li>A closer relationship with scholarly writing-in-progress than conventional publication allows</li>
          <li>Watching an argument develop, falter, and reconstitute across months</li>
          <li>Work positioned against premature closure — no artificial resolution</li>
          <li>Cross-project resonance: projects in the same season often respond to each other unknowingly</li>
          <li>An entry point that is always open, regardless of where in the season you arrive</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="cr-callout">
      <div class="cr-callout-title">A note on entry</div>
      <div class="cr-callout-text">You do not need to have been here from the beginning. Each installment is written to be legible on its own, while gaining depth when read in sequence. The archive rewards return, but does not require it. <em>Start anywhere.</em></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
