      <div class="about-col-label">(a) What we do</div>
      <div class="about-col-title">A system, not a journal</div>
      <p class="about-body-text"><em>The Unfinished Archive</em> is a serial research publication that develops scholarly and critical writing over time. It operates through seasonal cycles of 3–4 months, in which selected contributors publish monthly installments of work-in-progress.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">Each installment is a distinct, published piece of writing — positioned within a project sequence, dated, and held in place within the archive. Revisions are not corrections; they are new positions that coexist with the old ones.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">At the end of a cycle, projects may conclude, continue, or transition into <em>Second Form Editions</em>, the publication's book imprint. None of these outcomes is privileged. The archive exists to support the process, not to produce the object.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">The publication is free to read. Contributors are not charged. The archive is permanent and publicly accessible.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="about-col">
      <div class="about-col-label">(b) Why we exist</div>
      <div class="about-col-title">Against premature closure</div>

      <div class="manifesto-pull">Incompletion is not a failure of discipline. It is a form of fidelity to the problem.</div>

      <p class="about-body-text">Academic publishing operates under a logic of completion. Work is submitted when finished, reviewed as a finished object, published as a finished product. The <em>process</em> of arriving at a position is erased. What remains is the claim, stripped of its history.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">This logic produces a particular kind of knowledge — confident, bounded, premature. It forecloses the questions that could not survive the pressure to conclude.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">The Unfinished Archive proceeds from a different premise: that the movement of thought is as valuable as its resting points. That revision is not weakness but method. That the time between positions — the interval — is part of the argument.</p>
      <p class="about-body-text">We do not publish unfinished work as a celebration of incompletion. We publish <em>ongoing</em> work because ongoing is what thought actually is, and because the current forms of scholarly publication make that condition invisible.</p>
