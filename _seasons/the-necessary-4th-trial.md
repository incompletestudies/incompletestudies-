Season 04 introduces the core themes of the Journal of Incomplete Studies: unfinishedness, infrastructure, and the politics of memory. It launches the Digital Archives Initiative, a long-term research platform exploring how digital cultural heritage can be preserved, accessed, and interpreted.

This season is structured around three installments:

1.  **The Archive as Infrastructure**– A conceptual introduction.

2.  **Format Obsolescence and Preservation** – A material exploration.

3.  **Access and Governance** – A political analysis.

Together, these installments establish the foundations for the journal's ongoing engagement with unfinished and provisional knowledge.

The season also features contributions from researchers in digital humanities, archival studies, and media theory, and includes a collaborative workshop on sustainable digital preservation.
