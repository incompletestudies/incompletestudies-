Svenia Peter is a researcher in digital humanities and archival studies, working at the intersection of media theory, infrastructure studies, and critical memory. She is the Principal Investigator of the Digital Archives Initiative and a core contributor to the Seriality and Return project.

Her work focuses on the political and material conditions of digital preservation, with particular attention to how access, ownership, and interpretation shape the memory of the present. She has published widely on digital archives, platform governance, and the temporalities of media.

Svenia is currently developing a collaborative framework for sustainable digital preservation that bridges institutional archives, community-led initiatives, and artistic practice. She is also co-editing a special issue on unfinished archives for the Journal of Incomplete Studies.
